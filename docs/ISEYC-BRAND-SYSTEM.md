# ISEYC Institutional Brand System

**Institution:** Initiative for Sustainable Evolution for Youth and Community  
**Primary tagline:** Empowering Youth, Shaping Communities  
**Civic intelligence line:** Understanding Public Needs. Interpreting Evidence. Strengthening Accountability.

## Identity architecture
ISEYC is the parent institutional identity. Departments and civic intelligence products are distinct experiences inside one institution, not competing brands.

- Institution: ISEYC
- Departments: Education & Capacity Building; Health & Well-being; Software & Technology; Civic Department
- Citizen entry: Civic Mandate Nigeria
- Interpretation: Civic Brain
- Evidence foundation: Civic Intelligence Observatory
- Internal coordination: Digital Operations Centre

## Official mark
Use the supplied official seal at `/iseyc-seal.svg`. It is authoritative and must not be redrawn, recoloured, distorted, cropped or replaced by generated artwork. The horizontal lockup at `/iseyc-wordmark.svg` pairs that seal with the approved institutional name and tagline. Use the seal for compact app icons; use the lockup for reports, covers and formal institutional headers.

## Department accents
Education: forest + warm paper. Health: teal `#287A70` + pale teal `#E5F1EE`. Software & Technology: graphite + forest + restrained mint. Civic: forest + restrained gold. Accents identify departments; they do not replace the parent palette.

## Composition
Use editorial serif display titles and system sans-serif interface typography. Use tabular numerals for data. Maintain a 4-point spacing rhythm, 8–12px card radii, thin borders, restrained shadows, generous whitespace and responsive single-column mobile layouts. Keep introductions concise; reveal methodology and caveats progressively.

## Trust and accessibility
Never invent activity, performance, coverage or statistics. Preserve publisher, reference period, geography, measurement type and limitations. Do not imply approval, dispatch, verification or resolution without a source record. Use labelled statuses, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, accessible contrast and 44px touch targets. Design for low bandwidth.

## Approval
The supplied official seal is the source of truth. This standard governs its application and does not create a competing emblem.
