# ISEYC institutional design system — Civic Brain

This document applies the institution-wide identity to Civic Brain. The cross-product source of truth is [`ISEYC-BRAND-SYSTEM.md`](ISEYC-BRAND-SYSTEM.md); this file must not introduce competing marks, taglines, palettes, or department names.

## Institutional identity

- Institution: Initiative for Sustainable Evolution for Youth and Community (ISEYC)
- Primary tagline: **Empowering Youth, Shaping Communities**
- Civic intelligence line: **Understanding Public Needs. Interpreting Evidence. Strengthening Accountability.**
- Product hierarchy: ISEYC is the institution; Civic Brain interprets published civic evidence; the Intelligence Observatory is the evidence foundation; Civic Mandate Nigeria is the citizen entry point; the Digital Operations Centre is internal coordination.
- Departments: Education & Capacity Building; Health & Well-being; Software & Technology; Civic Department.

## Official marks

Use the existing `public/iseyc-seal.svg` and `public/iseyc-wordmark.svg` assets. The supplied seal and wordmark are canonical. Do not redraw, recolour, crop, distort, replace, or approximate either mark. Use the wordmark for institutional headers and formal contexts; use the seal only where a compact mark is appropriate.

## Typography and interface

Use an editorial serif for display headings and a readable sans-serif for interface text; use tabular numerals for data. Preserve the existing local font fallbacks and avoid adding remote font dependencies without a performance need. Keep layouts calm and responsive, with clear hierarchy, concise introductions, progressive methodology and caveats, visible keyboard focus, labelled statuses, reduced motion, accessible contrast, and touch targets of at least 44px.

## Evidence and language

Civic Brain interprets published, source-attributed evidence; it does not establish independent verification, make political endorsements, or claim that referrals were delivered or resolved. Preserve publisher, period, geography, measurement type, source limitations, and uncertainty. Do not expose unpublished submitter information or imply that pilot records represent public opinion or national prevalence.
